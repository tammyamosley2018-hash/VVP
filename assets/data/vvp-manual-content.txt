ABOUT THIS MANUAL
Volume I is the training and certification manual: scope, standards, ethics, session workflow, practitioner language, assessment categories, correction testing, reassessment, stabilization, documentation, referral rules, practicum, and certification. Volume II is the client-facing intake/results packet. Volume III is the practitioner-only client energetic wellness record. This manual is educational, spiritual, energetic, symbolic, environmental, and practitioner-training in nature. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prescription, disease management, emergency care, licensed inspection, or legal advice.

SECTION 1: WELCOME & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
Vibrational Virtue Practice is a complementary energetic wellness discipline using structured radiesthetic assessment, resonance testing, pendulum observation, field mapping, and correction compatibility protocols. Core principle: the practitioner observes energetic response patterns. The practitioner does not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, replace licensed care, guarantee outcomes, or represent uncertainty as certainty. Workflow: prepare practitioner and tools; confirm consent and scope; connect to witness when appropriate; scan the field and establish baseline; identify priority imbalance; test compatible corrective support; apply support within scope; confirm correction through reassessment; seal, stabilize, close; document the session.

SECTION 2: SCOPE OF PRACTICE & LEGAL STANDARDS
Official scope: VVP uses radiesthetic tools, pendulum observation, resonance testing, field mapping, witness work, and correction compatibility testing to observe energetic patterns and support coherence. It does NOT diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, replace medical/mental health care, perform psychotherapy, provide emergency services, or replace licensed environmental/electrical/EMF/mold/radon/structural inspection. Practitioner MAY: observe field response, use calibrated tools, assess resonance compatibility, apply tested corrective supports within scope, confirm correction through reassessment, seal and stabilize, document, refer when concerns exceed scope. Practitioner MAY NOT: diagnose disease or mental illness; prescribe medical treatment/medication/herbs/supplements/detox protocols unless separately qualified and legally permitted; claim to cure or guarantee outcomes; test without consent; represent uncertainty as certainty; replace emergency/licensed care/inspection/safety testing. Required disclaimer: VVP is complementary energetic wellness, not medical/mental health care, diagnosis, prescription, treatment, or emergency service; clients are responsible for their own health decisions and should consult licensed professionals for medical symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, medication, mental health, environmental hazards, or urgent matters.

SECTION 3: PRACTITIONER ETHICS & CODE OF CONDUCT
Foundation: do no harm, do not deceive, do not manipulate, do not exceed scope. Core duties: obtain consent before testing/witness work/correction/remote work; respect privacy and confidentiality; use clear language, avoid fear-based claims; represent training status truthfully; avoid dependency-based business practices; protect course materials/IP; use tools only within training and authorization; refer when a client needs care outside VVP. Students may not scan, test, influence, correct, emit toward, witness-work on, or document another person/place/object/environment without consent and ethical alignment. Course materials must not be copied, distributed, uploaded to AI systems, public platforms, or competing curriculum without written permission.

SECTION 4: PRACTITIONER IDENTITY & PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS
A VVP practitioner works with vibrational qualities, resonance patterns, and field responses within VVP scope. Be calm, clear, grounded; don't dramatize findings; don't use unearned titles/authority; don't call yourself instructor/representative unless formally authorized; don't use the work to manipulate or intimidate; know when to stop and refer. Approved status language: VVP student, VVP certification candidate, VVP practitioner, Certified VVP practitioner (only after certification), VVP instructor (only if authorized). Do not invent titles like master, healer, medical intuitive, inspector, licensor, affiliate, or authorized school.

SECTION 5: OFFICIAL VVP LANGUAGE & SERVICE SCRIPT
Public definition: VVP is a complementary energetic wellness and biofield harmonization discipline rooted in radiesthetic principles, field assessment, environmental sensitivity, and resonance-based correction. Required language: "This is an energetic observation," "This category tested as a priority," "This support tested as compatible," "This is not a diagnosis," "This does not replace licensed care," "Physical hazards need qualified evaluation." Prohibited language: "This cures you," "This proves disease," "Your organ is weak or failing," "Your house is toxic," "You are cursed," "Only I can fix this."

SECTION 6: SESSION WORKFLOW & STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES
Core session flow: clearing; tune to personal wavelength; confirm consent; connect to witness when appropriate; scan the field; identify priority imbalance; test compatible corrective support; apply correction within scope; confirm correction; reassess field response; seal and stabilize; document. Standard sequence: session prep; greeting and scope reminder; intake review and red flag check; consent confirmation; practitioner clearing/calibration; baseline field check; assessment category testing; priority ranking; correction compatibility testing; correction application/recommendation; reassessment; stabilization and witness closure; client education; documentation and referral notes. If the practitioner becomes unclear, the client becomes distressed, or a red flag appears: pause, stabilize, refer.

SECTION 7: RADIESTHESIA, TOOLS & CALIBRATION STANDARDS
Radiesthesia is disciplined field-response detection through controlled resonance — never treated as unlimited truth. Pendulum response meanings: strong clockwise = strong resonance/compatibility/support; slow clockwise = weak/mild support; counterclockwise = stress/blockage/incompatibility; back-and-forth = search position/neutral/unclear; no movement = blocked connection, recalibrate; erratic = interference/instability, stop and reset. Calibration rules: clear yourself, clear/prepare the tool, confirm consent, establish search position, tune to personal wavelength, ask one clear question at a time, use control questions, reassess after correction. Tool categories: neutral pendulum, Turenne-style pendulum, compass/orientation tools, witnesses, color witnesses, shape forms, anatomy/system charts, meridian/chakra charts, environmental maps/floor plans, advanced tools only after official training.

SECTION 8: INTAKE, CONSENT & WITNESS PROTOCOLS
No consent means no session. Intake must confirm: client identity/purpose, main concern, scope awareness, red flag check, consent for assessment/witness work/remote work, client right to pause or stop. A witness is a representation (name, photo, personal object, sample, map) used to establish radiesthetic connection with consent. Witness procedure: identify target, confirm consent, select simplest witness, label/place it, tune pendulum, test only agreed scope, close witness work after session, return/store/dispose per consent, document handling. No third-party testing without appropriate consent or legal authority.

SECTION 9: TERRAIN ASSESSMENT & PRIORITIZATION
Terrain categories: general vitality, hydration resonance, grounding response, lymphatic flow, detoxification stress pattern, rest/recovery pattern, emotional load, nervous system stress pattern, environmental burden, correction readiness. Use energetic language ("hydration support tested compatible," "grounding tested as priority") never medical claims ("toxic," "dehydrated," "diseased"). Priority rule: don't assume the loudest finding is first priority — test priority order, begin with the foundational support that tests strongest.

SECTION 10: ORGAN, GLAND & SYSTEM ASSESSMENT
Energetic category testing, not medical diagnosis/testing/lab or imaging interpretation. Approved: "Liver category showed weak energetic resonance," "This is an energetic observation, not a medical conclusion." Prohibited: "You have liver disease," "Your kidneys are failing," "This confirms infection," "This will cure the organ." Procedure: confirm consent/scope, use clean charts, calibrate, test general category then specific categories one at a time, rank priority, test correction compatibility, reassess, document, refer medical symptoms to licensed care. Spinal resonance assignments are energetic mapping tools only — a vertebral-category response does not diagnose an organ condition.

SECTION 11: BIOFIELD, CHAKRA & MERIDIAN ASSESSMENT
Do not use biofield/chakra language to diagnose trauma, mental illness, possession, curses, spiritual attack, disease, morality, or destiny. Major energy centers: Root (grounding/safety/stability), Sacral (flow/creativity/emotion), Solar Plexus (will/personal power), Heart (coherence/compassion), Throat (expression/communication), Third Eye (perception/insight), Crown (spiritual connection/higher alignment). Meridians assessed as energetic pathways ("weak flow response," "priority pathway") — never "the organ itself is diseased." Subtle-field work must close with grounding, breath, water, centering, witness closure, documentation.

SECTION 12: ENVIRONMENTAL, GEOPATHIC & EMF ASSESSMENT
Physical-first rule: mold, radon, wiring, carbon monoxide, gas leaks, moisture, unsafe water, structural concerns must be handled by qualified professionals — VVP environmental assessment does not replace inspection or emergency services. Bedroom-first assessment: bed location/headboard wall, phone/charger/router/mesh node/smart meter/breaker panel/appliances/heavy wiring near sleep zones, dampness/staining/musty odor/mold/ventilation/radon risk, move devices away from bed when practical, move the bed if it tests poorly and observe 14-30 days. Wireless audit zones: body, bed (within six feet of head), room, wall (behind headboard), children's beds, work area, outside (meters/transformers/towers). Reduce exposure without panic: distance, relocation, turning off devices, wiring what can be wired, observing changes.

SECTION 13: CORRECTION TESTING & COMPATIBILITY PROTOCOLS
Correction testing identifies energetically compatible support — not prescription, no guaranteed results. Sequence: assess, identify category stress, rank priority, confirm correction testing is appropriate, test possible supports, choose strongest compatible support, apply/recommend within scope, reassess, stabilize, document. Categories: foundational (water, grounding, breath, rest, reduced stimulation), field (centering vibration, color witness, shape form, chakra/meridian support), environmental (move bed/router, reduce wireless load, physical referral), advanced (crystal tools, Chestahedron, conscious emission, Melanin Pendulum — only if trained/authorized). Compatibility scale: strong clockwise=compatible, slow clockwise=mild/partial, counterclockwise=incompatible/stress, back-and-forth=neutral/unclear, erratic=stop/clear/recalibrate.

SECTION 14: CORRECTIONS, REASSESSMENT & STABILIZATION
Correction isn't complete until applied/recommended with consent, reassessed, stabilized, and documented. Sequence: state original priority, state compatible support, confirm consent, apply/recommend, observe response, reassess original priority, stabilize, document. Every correction must be reassessed — reassessment asks whether the field response changed, not whether the client was "cured." Stabilization protocol: put tools down, feet on floor, three slow breaths, offer water, state active testing is complete, close witness connection, confirm client feels steady, give simple home support, document stabilization.

SECTION 15: DOCUMENTATION STANDARDS OVERVIEW
Volume I teaches documentation standards; Volume III carries the full practitioner-only record; Volume II carries client-facing intake/results. Required session record elements: client name/code, date/session type, consent confirmation, red flag check, witness type, tools used, calibration notes, assessment categories, priority findings, corrections tested, reassessment results, stabilization, client education, referral if needed, witness handling. Write factual scope-safe language ("heart center weak clockwise; green witness compatible; reassessment strong clockwise") never conclusions like "client's heart is healed."

SECTION 16: CLIENT EDUCATION & HOME SUPPORT GUIDELINES
Client education rule: give one primary support, one secondary support, one observation instruction, one referral reminder when needed — don't overwhelm the client. Home support MAY include: hydration, grounding, rest, breath practice, journaling, centering practice, environmental observation, wireless reduction basics, color witness/shape form (only if tested compatible), follow-up. Home support MUST NOT include: medication instructions, supplement/herb prescriptions, disease or mental-health treatment plans, diagnosis-based advice, instructions to stop licensed care, environmental hazard clearance without qualified inspection.

SECTION 17: REFERRAL GUIDELINES & RED FLAG PROTOCOLS
Referral is professional responsibility, not failure. Medical red flags: chest pain, severe shortness of breath, stroke-like symptoms, fainting, seizure, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, severe abdominal pain, possible poisoning, any urgent/severe symptom. Mental health red flags: suicidal thoughts, self-harm, homicidal thoughts, psychosis, severe panic/depression/dissociation, client unable to stabilize. Environmental red flags: carbon monoxide alarm, gas smell, burning smell, hot outlets/sparking, electrical shocks, visible mold/musty odor with symptoms, water intrusion, radon concern, unsafe water, structural instability. Never use the pendulum to override referral, emergency care, medication guidance, or physical safety concerns.
